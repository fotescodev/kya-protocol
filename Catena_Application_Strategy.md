### "How do you think about agent identity?"

"The KYC paradigm doesn't translate to agents. You can't ask an AI for a passport. But you need verifiable answers to: Who authorized this agent? What can it do? Who's liable when it fails? I've designed a four-schema system—Identity, Capability, Provenance, Delegation—that composes to answer those questions cryptographically. The key insight is that every agent identity chain must terminate at a human or organization. Accountability is non-negotiable."

### "What would you build first at Catena?"

"I'd want to understand your current identity infrastructure, but my hypothesis is that Catena needs a trust layer that external services can query. When a merchant receives payment from a Catena-powered agent, they need to verify: Is this agent authorized? Is there a liable principal? Are there spending constraints? That verification should be instant, cryptographic, and portable across services. That's what KYA provides."
